Masala Omelette
Ingredients: 3 eggs, 1 chopped onion, 1 green chilli, a pinch of turmeric, salt, oil.
Method: Beat the eggs with the salt and turmeric. Heat a tablespoon of oil in a pan and fry the onion and chilli for two minutes. Pour in the eggs and cook on a medium flame until set. Fold and serve hot with buttered toast. Serves two. Preparation time: 10 minutes.
