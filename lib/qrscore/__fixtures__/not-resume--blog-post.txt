Five habits that helped me stay productive while working from home
When I first started working remotely, I struggled to keep a routine. In this post I want to share what finally worked for me. First, I set a fixed start time every morning, because without it the day just drifts away. Second, I take a proper lunch break away from the screen. Third, I keep my phone in another room while I work. These small changes have made a big difference, and I hope they help you too.
