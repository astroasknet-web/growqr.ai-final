Research Proposal: Adaptive learning systems for rural schools
Background. Many rural schools lack trained teachers. Objectives. To design and evaluate an adaptive learning platform for mathematics. Methodology. A mixed methods study with 12 schools over two years. Expected outcomes. Improved test scores and teacher confidence. Timeline. Year 1 design, year 2 evaluation. Budget. The total estimated budget is Rs 18 lakh. References are listed at the end of this proposal. This introduction sets out the problem and the conclusion summarises the plan.
