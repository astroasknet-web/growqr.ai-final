How to write a great resume in 2026
Your resume is your first impression. Keep it to one page, use a clean font and list your most recent job first. Many people wonder whether to include a photo; in India it is common but not required. In this guide we explain how to write a summary, how to choose the right skills and how to describe your experience with strong verbs.
