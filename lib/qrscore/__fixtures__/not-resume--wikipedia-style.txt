The Brihadisvara Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Shiva located in Thanjavur in the south Indian state of Tamil Nadu. It is one of the largest South Indian temples and an exemplary example of a fully realized Dravidian architecture. Built by Chola emperor Raja Raja Chola I between 1003 and 1010 CE, the temple is a part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site known as the Great Living Chola Temples. The temple stands amidst fortified walls that were added in the 16th century. According to historians, the vimana is 66 metres high.
