Abstract
This study examines the effect of spaced repetition on vocabulary retention among secondary school students. A randomized controlled trial was conducted with 240 participants across six schools. Keywords: spaced repetition, vocabulary, retention, secondary education.

1. Introduction
Vocabulary acquisition is a central concern of language pedagogy (Nation, 2013). Previous work has shown that distributed practice outperforms massed practice (Cepeda et al., 2006). In this paper we extend that finding to a classroom setting.

2. Methodology
Participants were assigned to a treatment or control group. Results and discussion are presented in Section 4. Conclusion: spaced practice improved retention by 18% (Fig. 2).
