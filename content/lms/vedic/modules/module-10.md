## Application 1: Shopping & Billing

### Scenario: Grocery Store Calculation

You buy: 3 items at ₹47 each, 2 items at ₹98 each, 4 items at ₹25 each.

**Vedic approach:**
- 3 × 47 = 141 (using 3×50−3×3=150−9=141)
- 2 × 98 = 196 (using 2×100−2×2=200−4=196)
- 4 × 25 = 100
- Total = 141 + 196 = 337, +100 = 437

**Practice Problem:** Calculate total for 5 items at ₹96, 3 items at ₹105, 6 items at ₹48.

**C2.** [3 marks] Multiply 97 × 96 using the Nikhilam method. Show all steps.

*Answer:*

Deficiencies: 100−97=3, 100−96=4

0+5=5 → Q₄=5

**C5.** [3 marks] Explain the difference between conventional and Vedic multiplication for 98 × 97.

*Answer:*

**C8.** [3 marks] A product is 123 × 45 = 5x35. Find the missing digit x using digital roots.

*Answer:*

**C10.** [3 marks] What is the nine-point circle? Draw and explain.

*Answer:*
The nine-point circle is a circular arrangement of digits 1 through 9 (with 9 at the top representing both 9 and 0 modulo 9). Moving clockwise adds 1, counterclockwise subtracts 1. Adding 9 brings you back to the same point. It visualizes digital roots and modular arithmetic.

```
                   9 (or 0)
                /           \
              8              1
             /                 \
            7        ●          2
             \                 /
              6              3
                \           /
                   5 ——— 4
```
